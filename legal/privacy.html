Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-20 Owner-operated, single-user software

Project-Sunny is a personal assistant application built, hosted, and used by one person — its owner. It is not a company, not a commercial product, and not offered to the public. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your Google data if you're the owner connecting your own account.

01 What Sunny is

Sunny is a self-hosted personal assistant that runs on hardware the owner controls. It has exactly one user: the person who built it. There is no sign-up, no accounts for other people, and no distribution to anyone else. If you're reading this because you're being asked to grant Google account access, that access is to power the owner's own private instance of this software — not a service being offered to you.

02 What it accesses, and why

With the owner's permission via Google Sign-In, Sunny reads two things, and only reads them:

calendar.readonlyUpcoming events, so a morning/evening briefing can mention what's on the calendar.
gmail.readonlyRecent messages, so a briefing can surface what's new or needs attention.

Sunny never requests permission to send email, modify or delete calendar events, or take any action on your Google account. Read access only.

03 Where the data goes

Nowhere but back to the owner. Calendar and Gmail data is fetched directly from Google's own APIs over an authenticated connection, used to generate a briefing, and shown only inside the owner's private web console — a page that isn't publicly reachable and has no other viewers.

No third parties. Nothing is sold, shared, licensed, or transmitted to any advertiser, analytics provider, or data broker. There is no analytics or tracking pixel running anywhere in Sunny.

04 Where it's stored, and for how long

The OAuth token that lets Sunny read the owner's Calendar and Gmail is stored locally, on the owner's own server, alongside the rest of the application's data. It is kept only until the owner revokes it — either from inside Sunny's console ("Disconnect Google Account") or from Google's own Account Permissions page, either of which takes effect immediately.

05 Changes to this policy

If what Sunny accesses or how it's used ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change with it. Since there's one user, that user will already know.

06 Contact

Questions about this policy or Sunny's use of your Google data can be sent to keatonstuff@gmail.com.
