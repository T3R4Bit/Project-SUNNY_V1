Home

A personal assistant, built by one person, for one person.

Project-Sunny runs on hardware its owner controls, answers to nobody but them, and isn't offered as a service to anyone else.

Purpose: Project-Sunny is a self-hosted home assistant application. It delivers daily briefings (weather, calendar, tasks), tracks tasks/lists/reminders, and — with the owner's Google sign-in — reads the owner's own Calendar and Gmail (read-only) to include in those briefings. It has one user: its owner.

01 What it does

Sunny handles the everyday stuff a home assistant should: morning and evening briefings, tasks and lists, alarms and reminders, and — where connected — a quick read on what's coming up in Calendar and what's new in Gmail. Most of it runs on plain pattern matching, not a language model; the owner can talk to it through a private web console or a Discord bot in their own server.

Briefings

Weather, calendar, and tasks, summarized on request.

Tasks & Lists

Stored as plain markdown, readable outside the app too.

Alarms & Reminders

Deterministic time parsing, delivered over Discord.

Calendar & Gmail

Read-only, only for the owner's own account.

02 Who this is for

Just the owner. There's no sign-up, no other accounts, and no plan to open it up — it's a personal project, not a product. If you've landed here from a Google sign-in screen, that's because you are the owner, connecting your own Google account to your own private instance.
